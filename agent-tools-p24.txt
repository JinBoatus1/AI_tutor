Proof
It is H um an to  seek proof, to ask why. If your neighbor says evacuate because of hurricanes, wouldn t you 
seek verification? If you claim to  have a perpetual m otion machine, I won’t take you at your word. We each 
have different thresholds to be convinced of something. In life, a few true instances are often enough - that

m orning.” It only m eans the statem ent is not obviously false. In m athem atics, we have the same urge for 
verification, b u t our expectations are high. We require deductive proof.
